# Northwind Robotics Employee Handbook

*This is a fictional document, written for testing a chatbot. Northwind Robotics is not a real company.*

Version 4.2. Northwind Robotics builds warehouse robots and has offices in Leeds (headquarters), Rotterdam and Kraków. This handbook applies to all employees. Where a rule is different for contractors, interns or part-time staff, it says so.

## 1. Working hours

The standard working week is 37.5 hours, Monday to Friday. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00, when everyone is expected to be available. Outside core hours you may start and finish when you like, as long as you work your contracted hours.

Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager. Approved overtime is paid at 1.5 times your hourly rate on weekdays and 2 times your hourly rate on weekends and public holidays. Instead of payment, you may choose time off in lieu, at the same multipliers.

## 2. Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Interns receive 1.5 days of leave for each full month worked.

Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March; anything not used by then is forfeited.

Leave requests of up to 3 days need at least one week's notice. Requests of more than 3 days need at least four weeks' notice. Leave is requested in the HR portal, called "Harbor".

After 5 full years of service, employees receive 2 extra days of annual leave per year (26 days in total for full-time staff).

## 3. Sick leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. If you are ill, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day. For an absence longer than 3 consecutive working days, a doctor's note is required. Sick days do not carry over into the next year.

## 4. Parental leave

Birth parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Partners and adoptive parents receive 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must be requested at least 8 weeks before it starts. Employees become eligible for paid parental leave after 6 months of employment.

## 5. Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. The other days are spent in the office, and every team has one fixed "anchor day" per week when everyone is in the office together.

Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per year, and must be approved by HR at least two weeks in advance.

Every permanent employee receives a one-off home-office allowance of €400 to buy a desk, chair or other equipment for working from home. Contractors and interns are not eligible for the home-office allowance.

## 6. Expenses

Business expenses are reimbursed when they are submitted with a receipt in the expenses tool within 30 days of the purchase. Expenses submitted later than 30 days are not reimbursed.

Meals while travelling for work are covered up to €45 per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Train travel is preferred for trips under 5 hours; flights must be economy class. Any single expense over €500 needs approval from a director before you buy it.

Business mileage in your own car is reimbursed at €0.30 per kilometre.

## 7. Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop on their first day: engineers get a 16-inch laptop with 64 GB of memory, and everyone else gets a 14-inch laptop with 32 GB of memory. Laptops are replaced every 3 years.

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours. You may keep using your own phone for work email if it has the company's device-management app installed.

## 8. Security

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is required for every company system. Never share your password, not even with IT; IT will never ask for it.

Visitors to any office must sign in at reception and be accompanied by an employee at all times. The robot test floor in Leeds requires safety boots and a completed safety course; visitors are not allowed on the test floor.

Report suspected phishing emails using the "Report phish" button in your email client. Security incidents must be reported to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of discovery.

## 9. Learning and development

Each employee has a learning budget of €1,200 per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused learning budget does not carry over. Employees may also spend 4 hours per month of working time on learning.

Northwind pays for one professional certification per year, as long as it is relevant to your role and approved by your manager.

## 10. Onboarding

New employees are assigned an onboarding buddy from another team for their first 3 months. During the first week, every new employee completes security training and, if they will work near robots, the safety course. The probation period is 6 months for permanent employees.

## 11. Benefits

Northwind contributes 6% of your salary to your pension, as long as you contribute at least 3% yourself. Employees get private health insurance from their first day. There is a €50 monthly gym allowance. Contractors are not eligible for the pension contribution, the health insurance or the gym allowance.

## 12. Leaving Northwind

The notice period is 1 month during probation and 3 months after probation. Unused annual leave is paid out when you leave. Company laptops and badges must be returned on your last day.
